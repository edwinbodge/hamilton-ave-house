How this model was made
  • Walls, doors, windows and rooms come from the listing's Zillow 3D-tour floor map, which is drawn to scale in meters from the 3D scan, so walls are within a few inches. Each floor's map has its own origin; the 2nd floor was aligned on the living room it opens over (back wall, west edge and the chimney chase) and the stair, the basement on its side walls and the dining bump.
  • The 2nd floor reaches about 4½ ft past the 1st floor's front wall, over the porch, with a short pent roof below it (the front photos show the same).
  • Heights are assumed: 9 ft main-floor ceilings, 10 ft floor to floor (16 stair risers), 8 ft upstairs, basement floor 9.4 ft down with 8.4 ft ceilings, garage slab 6" below the house.
  • The stair is a U: up from the living room toward the front, a landing over the mudroom, and back up to the loft; the basement stair runs under it. The listing's "stairs" labels on the 1st floor are swapped relative to where the other two floors put them, so this follows the floors above and below.
  • Roofs are read from the photos and the satellite view: a side-gabled main roof (10/12) whose back slope comes down over the vaulted living room and kitchen, a dormer at the back, a front gable over bedroom 3, a hipped primary suite, a gabled dining bump, and stepped garage gables (7/12).
  • Compass: the house faces north onto Susan Ln, the garage is on the east, and the back yard faces south. The lot is drawn as about 82 × 216 ft (0.41 ac) from the satellite view. Get the plat of survey before planning near a lot line.
  • Finishes follow the listing photos: brown LVP, taupe walls, blue primary bedroom, white shaker cabinets and doors, black granite, stacked-stone fireplace, tan fiber-cement siding with shakes in the gables and a stone wainscot.
