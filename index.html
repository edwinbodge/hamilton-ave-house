How this model was made
  • Walls, doors, windows and room sizes come from the listing's CubiCasa floor plans (1st floor 23.55 px/ft, 2nd 36.75, basement 27.2). Expect a few inches of error.
  • Floors are aligned on the main stair and the fireplace chase.
  • Assumed heights: 8 ft ceilings on both floors, 9 ft floor to floor, ~7′5″ basement drop ceiling, vaulted sun room.
  • Roof pitches are estimated from the photos: main hip 7/12, garage 8/12, sun room 10/12, front & rear gables 12/12.
  • The lot is drawn as a 99.5 × 170.6 ft rectangle (0.39 ac) from the aerial. The real Harrison Ave line curves, and the house's exact setbacks are estimated. Get a plat of survey before planning anything near a lot line.
  • Wall colors, wallpaper, trim, cabinets, tile and fixtures follow the listing photos. Door swings follow the floor plans.
  • Furniture is staged at real sizes for scale: king bed in the primary, queen in the 11′6″ bedroom, full in the 10′10″ bedroom, a 6-seat dining table, and two cars in the garage. Turn it off under Show.
