How this model was made
  • There are no floor plans yet. The outside comes from the county parcel (the lot is 66 × 132 ft), the listing aerial for where the roofs sit (good to about 2 ft), and 25 photos taken on site. The inside is fitted to the listing's 19 interior photos, its room sizes, and the owner's description of the layout. Expect rooms to be off by a foot or two until the plans arrive.
  • The house is three parts: the steep front gable (living room, front bedroom), the steep cross gable behind it (family room, stair, kitchen; bedroom 3 and the primary bedroom above), and the later two-story rear part with the low gable (bath, laundry, back entry; primary bath and office above). The breakfast nook is the enclosed west end of the front porch.
  • Heights are assumed: first floor 2.4 ft above grade (four porch risers), 8½ ft ceilings, 9½ ft floor to floor (15 stair risers), 4.8 ft knee walls under the steep roofs (13/12) and 6.3 ft under the rear roof. The back entry is a landing at grade with three steps up to the kitchen.
  • Guesses: the basement stair (drawn under the main stair, from the door in the living room), the upstairs hall and closets, which rooms the unphotographed doors lead to, the basement itself, and the neighbors' houses.
  • Compass: the house faces east onto Mequon Ave; the driveway runs along the south side. The lot is drawn square to the compass (it is really about 1.3° off).
  • The new garages are taller than Cedarburg's 20 ft limit for detached buildings and larger than its 720 sq ft limit, so any of them would need Plan Commission approval. Setbacks drawn: 3.5 ft from lot lines, 10 ft or more from the house.
