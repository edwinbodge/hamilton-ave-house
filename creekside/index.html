How this model was made
  • Walls, doors, windows and room sizes come from the listing's CubiCasa plans. Scale: 1st floor 12.1 px/ft, set from the garage (23′3″ × 28′1″), family room (18′4″ × 20′2″), dining and kitchen dimensions on the dimensioned plan in the photo set, which agree within 1%. Basement 12.95 px/ft. Expect a few inches of error.
  • The 2nd floor is drawn only on the small three-floor plan (5.95 px/ft). It was scaled from the same image as the 1st floor and aligned on the two-story family room walls and the stair. Bedroom 2 (12′2″ × 14′2″) and 3 (12′2″ × 13′9″) and the primary bedroom (23′2″ × 15′9″) match the dimensioned plan.
  • The basement was aligned on the kitchen bump, chimney/utility chase, front wall and the east room. Its rear walls under the dining area and office land about 2 ft inside the 1st-floor walls, so those two rooms are drawn as cantilevered.
  • Heights are assumed: 9 ft 1st-floor ceilings, 10 ft floor to floor (15 stair risers), 8 ft upstairs, 8 ft basement ceiling, family room sunken one step (photos). The family room vault is the underside of the main roof's back slope, which carries on down over the family room.
  • Roof shapes and pitches are read from the photos and aerials: main hip 9/12, the bay tower's hip, the bedroom bump over the garage, garage hip at the front with a rake at the back, 7/12. Two skylights (primary bath, upstairs hall).
  • Compass: the front is assumed to face east (from shadows in the photos), so the sun study is approximate. The lot is drawn as a 96 × 131 ft rectangle (0.29 ac) with the house placed from the aerials. Get the plat of survey before planning near a lot line.
  • Finishes follow the listing photos: white walls, gray living room with a chair rail and tray ceiling, golden oak trim, doors and cabinets, gray laminate counters, black appliances, black marble fireplace, beige foyer tile, mauve living room carpet, pink carpet in bedroom 3, tan brick and taupe lap siding outside.
  • Furniture is staged at real sizes, roughly as in the photos (queen in the primary, twin and full in the bedrooms, two cars in the garage). Turn it off under Show.
